The positivity b-2a follows from strict Hong--Krahn--Szegő on the connected disk. The polynomial trials and J_0 Rayleigh sum give a<295/51, a>4*sqrt(2), b<16. Hence the direct k=3 exclusion width is <2^-18, while the full interval has width 12-2a>22/51. All rational/integer verifier checks were rerun successfully. ### Sol 3: the actual gluing obstruction

For a Lipschitz radial Weinberger field on each Voronoi cell, H^1 membership requires matching traces on every active interface. On a bisector the center vectors differ by the reflection R_ij. Their values on a relatively open face span R^d: an affine hyperplane at nonzero distance from the center has full linear span, and multiplication by positive scalars does not change that span.

Thus Q_i=Q_j R_ij. Substituting successively gives
R_31 R_23 R_12=I; taking inverses gives the order written in Sol 3. Both have determinant -1, contradiction. A fixed-domain claim requires all three faces to meet the open domain; a boundary-only contact is not an active interface. Singular/variable frames and genuinely acyclic active adjacencies are not excluded. The 3d moment reduction itself is correct, including eigenvalue multiplicity.

### Sol 4: endpoint-safe tensorization

The positive layer-cake identity is valid for every gamma>0; endpoint eigenvalue levels have zero measure in the integral. Tensor sums are finite below fixed E. Dirichlet inclusive counts at residual zero are zero; Neumann strict counts at residual zero are zero even though the Riesz sum at positive energy includes mu_0. Separation of the closed quadratic forms gives the full product spectrum for bounded Lipschitz factors, including interval factors and corners. No Neumann domain-monotonicity assumption is used.

The beta identity C_b L_(b/2,a)^cl=C_(a+b) is exact. The half-Riesz localization claim follows since the Abel kernel has integral sqrt(E). These are valid reductions, not unsmoothing of an arbitrary positive-order bound.
