### 1. Exact actual-domain cylinder reduction

For a fixed admissible base `Omega` and interval length `L`, separation of the quadratic-form eigenbases gives

\[
N_{D,L}^{\le}(E)=\sum_{\lambda_j\le E}\left\lfloor\frac L\pi\sqrt{E-\lambda_j}\right\rfloor,
\qquad
N_{N,L}^{<}(E)=\sum_{\mu_j<E}\left\lceil\frac L\pi\sqrt{E-\mu_j}\right\rceil.
\]

The rounding error is bounded by the finite relevant base count. Hence Pólya for **all lengths** of these cylinders is equivalent, separately for each boundary condition, to the sharp half-Riesz bound

\[
R_{1/2}^D(E)\le\pi C_{d+1}|\Omega|E^{(d+1)/2},
\qquad
R_{1/2}^N(E)\ge\pi C_{d+1}|\Omega|E^{(d+1)/2}.
\]

For a planar base the coefficient is `|Omega|/(6*pi)`. The limit `L->infinity` is justified at fixed energy by the explicit finite rounding bound; no uniform limit is assumed. An actual half-Riesz violation of size `delta` transfers to a finite cylinder with `L>pi*N(E)/delta`. A Dirichlet excess witnessed using `m` terms needs only `L>pi*m/delta` and certified upper bounds for those eigenvalues. A Neumann deficit needs control of the complete sum and a certified tail cutoff.

This is an elementary rigorous reduction, not a claimed novel theorem or a solution of arbitrary-domain Pólya. Full proofs and an enclosure recipe appear in the [cumulative TeX note](../artifacts/2026-09-08_research_notes.tex) and [product note](../artifacts/spectral_attack_yang_and_cylinders_2026-09-08.md).

### 2. A sharp obstruction even below order one

In normalized planar units, take `a_j=j+ceil(sqrt(j))` and lower precisely entries `90,...,100` to `399/4`. The resulting nondecreasing positive sequence has `b_100<100` and `N(E)=E-sqrt(E)+O(1)`, yet satisfies

\[
\sum_j(E-b_j)_+^\sigma\le\frac{E^{\sigma+1}}{\sigma+1}
\quad\text{for every } E\ge0,\ \sigma\ge1/128,
\qquad \sum_j e^{-tb_j}\le1/t.
\]

The proof is analytic for all energies and all these orders. Its key local compensation is a counting deficit at least 7 on `[199/4,399/4)`, against an excess at most `1/4` on `[399/4,100)`. A kernel-ratio lemma reduces the cutoff to the exact integer inequality `29^128<=201*28^128`. More generally every prescribed positive cutoff `s` has a countermodel with `K=O(s^(-2))`. This is a cutoff-dependent family; a single spectrum satisfying all positive orders down to zero would satisfy counting and is not claimed.

The [proof and local deficit lemma](../artifacts/obstruction_referee_2026-09-08.md) are independently checked in this invocation; the [exact verifier](../artifacts/obstruction_referee_2026-09-08.py) passed.

### 4. The geometric term that scalar estimates omit

Let `T_E` be the compression to `L^2(Omega)` of the Fourier projection onto the energy ball, and let `P_E` be the Dirichlet spectral projection. The exact trace identity is

\[
C_d|\Omega|E^{d/2}-N_D^{\le}(E)
=\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits((I-P_E)T_E)-\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits(P_E(I-T_E)).
\]

It compares high-mode Fourier mass inside the ball with low-mode mass outside. Both traces are finite; the derivation uses zero extension and Hilbert–Schmidt kernels, not extra boundary regularity. The tempting stronger inequality `T_E>=P_E` is impossible when `P_E` is nonzero: a nonzero compactly supported function cannot be bandlimited. This identity is a reformulation, not an established favorable sign. It identifies an actual geometric compensation estimate for further attack.
