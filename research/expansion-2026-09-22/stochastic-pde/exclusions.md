# Screening decisions — stochastic and nonlocal PDEs

Checked on 22 September 2026. These are discovery and editorial records, outside the active count. A reported proof is not treated as independently verified merely because it is available online.

- **Unforced periodic Navier–Stokes:** retained existing entry 073 after checking that the September 2026 Navier–Stokes manuscript instead constructs a smooth external force. Its statement is not silently identified with the unforced problem.
- **Maxwell–Stefan mixtures with additive friction:** [Bothe, September 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06732) proves global strong solutions under the additive-friction structure. The admitted problem keeps arbitrary symmetric friction and at least four species.
- **Three-dimensional nonlocal Cahn–Hilliard separation:** resolved in [Poiatti, Analysis & PDE 18 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.2140/apde.2025.18.109). The new separation question uses the local Laplacian in the chemical potential.
- **Fractional minimal cones near zero interaction order:** the [August 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21340) treats sufficiently small order. Together with the near-local theorem it leaves an intermediate range; the catalogue asks for all orders in one entry.
- **Fractional-pressure porous-medium uniqueness in one dimension:** the [2026 FoCM article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10208-026-09752-y) concerns one dimension. The admitted uniqueness question concerns several dimensions. Uniqueness of a discrete minimizing step is also not uniqueness of the evolving weak solution.
- **Critical scalar-mass conservation from the 2023 nonlinear-diffusion survey:** some initially promising subsections already contain their own resolutions; those were not admitted merely because the introduction uses the language of open questions.
- **General Liouville theorems for fully nonlinear equations:** [Li–Liang (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19075) and [Liang (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10797) require convexity/concavity in the relevant dimensions. The admitted bounded-Hessian rigidity question has no such assumption.
- **Bourgain–Spencer homogenization at small contrast:** [Duerinckx–Lemm–Pagano](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09909) establishes the perturbative conjecture. The finite-contrast problem remains and is stated with a concrete Gaussian coefficient model and an explicit expansion.
- **Stable cone profiles with sufficiently integrable angular densities:** [Ros-Oton–Weidner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01695) proves uniqueness with an angular-integrability hypothesis. The admitted question allows singular spectral measures, retaining ellipticity and growth bounds.
- **Stochastic De Giorgi regularity with smooth transport:** [Agresti–Sauerbrey–Veraar](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.12692) proves a sample-dependent Hölder exponent. Its explicit remaining questions on bounded measurable noise and a deterministic exponent are distinguished. The further moment-bound question was not counted as a third similar entry.
- **Stochastic quadratic reaction–diffusion under triangular structure:** [Milesis–Salins (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06645) assumes additional structure and has no derivative in its noise. [Agresti–Kniely–Tang (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13332) selects a regularizing transport noise and obtains arbitrarily high probability. Neither establishes almost-sure global existence for every admissible transport noise.
- **No-slip stochastic fluid weak existence:** [Goodair (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13199) gives martingale weak solutions. Navier-slip strong solutions and no-slip weak solutions are not a local pathwise-unique no-slip strong theory.
- **Stochastic fluid regularization with high probability:** [Agresti (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15140) chooses noise to obtain a high-probability result. This does not imply a universal critical-norm continuation criterion for an arbitrary fixed admissible noise.
- **Reaction–diffusion weak–strong uniqueness:** [Fischer (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.00730) and subsequent interface analogues do not imply unconditional uniqueness between renormalized solutions. Likewise, constructing a solution satisfying mass conservation does not prove that the truncated nonlinear-diffusion identity forces conservation for all solutions.
- **Chemical global attraction under special hypotheses:** a single reversible reaction, near-equilibrium data, sufficiently large diffusivity, or absence of boundary equilibria do not cover arbitrary complex-balanced spatial networks. The admitted asymptotic question assumes global boundedness and does not duplicate the separate classical-existence problem or existing ODE persistence entry 276.

Related questions about a single model were retained only when their conclusions differ materially: weak uniqueness, mass conservation, point-source existence, regularity, support propagation, stationary minimizers, and long-time attraction. Dimensions or parameter values of the same unresolved statement were kept together.

- **Critical quadratic-noise Allen–Cahn maximum moment:** [held before admission](held-allen-cahn-endpoint.md). The survey explicitly asks this question, but the second review identified a material concern about its spatially constant reduction. It was replaced without claiming a solved theorem or attempting a proof.
