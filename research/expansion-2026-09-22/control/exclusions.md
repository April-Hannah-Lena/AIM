# Excluded or narrowed control and geometric-PDE candidates

Status searches were performed through 22 September 2026. A recent matching claim was enough to exclude a candidate from the open collection; this log does not certify a new proof's correctness.

- Stable Bernstein in ambient dimension seven: a matching September 2026 claim, [arXiv:2609.15720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15720), makes the old unqualified question unsuitable.
- Schiffer/Pompeiu: matching August 2026 counterexample claims [arXiv:2608.05114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05114) and [arXiv:2608.01579](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01579); excluded.
- Pólya–Szegő electrostatic capacity at fixed surface area: matching [arXiv:2607.02273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02273); excluded.
- Planar Monge–Ampère W2,1 regularity under an upper determinant bound only: contradicted by C. Mooney, Analysis & PDE 9 (2016), 881–891, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2140/apde.2016.9.881). Retained the distinct two-sided-density sharp exponent question.
- Critical-length KdV small-time control classification: [arXiv:2501.13640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13640), revised December 2025, provides the classification. Retained odd-dimensional center-mode decay, explicitly left open in §2.4.5.
- KdV half-line control: matching 2026 article [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bulsci.2026.103909); excluded.
- Defocusing cubic NLS on the torus, global exponential stabilization: [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5802/afst.1819); excluded.
- Unbounded-input ISS implies integral ISS: [arXiv:2605.12121](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12121) resolves broad classes including reflexive spaces. It does not settle arbitrary-Banach bounded-input state continuity; only that separate question is retained.
- Boundary Burgers input-to-state stability: the 2024 Zheng–Zhu result addresses SIAM Review's earlier problem; excluded.
- Infinite-network small-gain sufficiency: the Mironchenko–Kawan–Glück result [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00498-021-00303-0) addresses the earlier review question; excluded.
- Delay-system forward completeness implying bounded reachable sets: 2024 counterexample found; excluded.
- Heat control with an interior inverse-square singularity: [arXiv:2505.07302](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07302) resolves the earlier Morancey question; excluded.
- Global Lipschitz regularity of Monge–Ampère eigenfunctions: [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5802/afst.1854), [arXiv:2501.01358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01358); excluded.
- Two-dimensional inhomogeneous Navier–Stokes weak uniqueness: [arXiv:2406.19907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19907); excluded.
- Optimal quadratic insulation with two free bodies and volume constraints: the concentric-ball theorem [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00526-022-02298-1) resolves the old problem. Retained distinct prescribed-thickness/perimeter and nonlinear-radiative budget questions.
- Sharp one-dimensional heat-control exponential coefficient: Lissy, [arXiv:2608.08041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08041), supplies a new matching determination. Retained the separate sharp Schrödinger coefficient and positive-transport flushing-time conjecture.
- The old negative-transport threshold 2L/|M| and heat coefficient 1/4: contradicted by Lissy's 2015 lower bounds. The retained transport entry explicitly fixes positive velocity.
- Fractional Schrödinger optimal exponential order: [arXiv:2601.12810](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12810) does not settle the leading coefficient for the classical one-dimensional Schrödinger boundary problem.
- KdV fast-control cost order: [arXiv:2602.12698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12698); excluded.
- Full anisotropic Maxwell recovery: substantial partial results found, but no sufficiently explicit source formulation was admitted in this batch.
- Variable-density Navier–Stokes global boundary control, complete Boussinesq control and one-control Stefan questions: excluded because the reviewed survey statements did not fix enough boundary, compatibility or coupling details for an unambiguous entry without additional source verification.
- Polynomial controllability-radius growth is retained as Jafarpour's distinct quantitative conjecture, not a restatement of existing problem148 on finite-jet robustness.
- The catalytic reaction conjecture's literal nonnegative-data wording admits a stationary boundary equilibrium. The retained question explicitly requires strictly positive initial concentrations.
- All numerical-linear-algebra candidates, including the control-book matrix questions, were excluded by scope.
