# Independent review of the vector Riesz replacement

Reviewed on 2026-09-22 by the control/inverse-PDE research agent.

**Outcome:** no blocking mathematical or open-status issue found in the admitted vector Riesz-transform entry.

- Reopened the full introduction of Bañuelos–Osękowski, JFA 269 (2015), [author text](https://arxiv.org/html/1305.1492). Equations (1.4)–(1.5) distinguish the Euclidean vector transform from its individual components. The paragraph immediately after (1.7) explicitly conjectures the draft's cotangent constant for the vector norm. Its domain is the full interval 1 < p < infinity; p = 2 is correctly identified as settled by Plancherel.
- Reopened Bañuelos's [2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09365), sections 5.1–5.2. Equation (5.1) uses the same Euclidean vector norm, Remark 5.1 explicitly distinguishes real inputs from complex inputs, and (5.13) gives the stated component lower bound. The paragraph following it says that the exact norm remains open even in dimension two for real inputs. The draft's restriction to real smooth compactly supported functions is precise and density gives the corresponding real Lp assertion.
- The Fourier multiplier's overall minus sign has no effect on the norm; the convention is internally explicit. With the stated Fourier convention, its potential interpretation is the negative gradient of the inverse square root of minus the Laplacian, so adding “up to sign” to the informal applied description would make that description literal.
- Reopened the [August 2026 weak-type paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18068). Its dimension-free weak-(1,1) result is different from the exact strong-Lp constant asked for here.

Minor reference-locator refinement: reference 2 can say “sections 5.1–5.2, especially (5.1), Remark 5.1, (5.13) and the following discussion,” because equation (5.1) occurs in section 5.1 rather than section 5.2.
