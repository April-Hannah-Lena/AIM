# Independent cross-review of the PDE batch

Reviewed 22 September 2026 by the control/transport reviewer.

Read all 35 draft statements, references and status reviews. Checked equation signs, boundary conditions, quantifiers, functional spaces, conservation constraints and separation from the existing catalogue and the control batch. No blocking mathematical formulation issue was identified. This is a source/formulation review, not an attempted solution or a guarantee that no later resolution exists.

Independently reopened these primary full-text passages:

- Bressan, *One Dimensional Hyperbolic Conservation Laws: Past and Future*, §7 Definition 7.1, Corollary 7.1 and Open Problem 3, plus §8 Open Problem 6. The draft correctly repairs the source's apparent terminal-test-time typo and distinguishes entropy admissibility from jump-only criteria.
- Barchiesi–Henao–Mora-Corral–Rodiac, *A Lavrentiev phenomenon in the neo-Hookean model*, §1.1 and Theorem 1.1. The gap between a regular class and its weak closure does not establish nonattainment of the infimum within the regular class. The draft preserves this distinction.
- Wang–Xiao–Xiong, arXiv:2603.16767v3, §1.1: explicit unresolved whole-space Maxwellian damping question; screened and polynomial-tail results are different.
- Berestycki–Graham, *A Stable-Compact Method*, §8.1 Open Question3 and §8.5 Open Question7(ii): these support strong-KPP uniqueness and the two-ball exterior problem as separate questions with different hypotheses.
- Fournier–Laurençot, *Marcus–Lushnikov processes, Smoluchowski's and Flory's models*, Assumption(Aα), Definition2.2, Proposition2.3 and the following discussion. The draft reproduces the weak Flory model and distinguishes continuity after gelation from continuity at its onset. The later bilinear model does not cover arbitrary kernels bounded by x^αy+xy^α.

Requested a typography correction in four integrals: cutoff Boltzmann continuation, universal hyperbolic error rate, arbitrary-Mach Boltzmann shocks, and Flory mass continuity had literal commas before differentials. The PDE author is correcting these in the source generator.

Particular formulation checks that passed: the relativistic Vlasov–Poisson sign is repulsive; the Schrödinger-map 8π degree-zero normalization is explicit; Zakharov small-energy scattering is distinct from large-data finite-time collapse; planar nonresistive MHD is distinct from ideal MHD; stress-defect removal is explicit in the Oldroyd and liquid-crystal entries; strong-KPP critical nonexistence is distinct from uniqueness conditional on existence; FENE zero-diffusion convergence is distinct from separate endpoint existence.

The four differential-spacing corrections were applied before integration.
