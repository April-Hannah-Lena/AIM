# Exclusions and status-sensitive comparisons — PDE batch

Reviewed through 2026-09-22. A preprint announcement is recorded as such; neither inclusion nor exclusion claims to certify a proof independently.

- **Planar Gross–Pitaevskii waves at all subsonic speeds:** [2609.08906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08906) announces the matching existence result. Excluded.
- **Homogeneous Coulomb Landau global regularity:** [Guillén–Silvestre 2311.09420](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09420), published in Acta Mathematica (2025), proves it. Included questions instead concern ancient rigidity and bounds using only macroscopic data.
- **Homogeneous noncutoff very-soft Boltzmann global smoothness:** [Imbert–Silvestre–Villani 2409.01183](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01183), Inventiones Mathematicae 243 (2026), includes the physical range in Theorem 1.6. Excluded.
- **Beyond-short-time dilute-gas derivation:** [2408.07818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07818) and [2503.01800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01800) by Deng–Hani–Ma materially change the long-time Lanford question. Excluded rather than restating an obsolete barrier.
- **Gaussian whole-space damping versus related solved models:** [2205.04540](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04540) proves nonlinear damping for Poisson tails, [2405.04473](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04473) treats the periodic endpoint Gevrey problem, and [2603.29204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29204) addresses weakly collisional dynamics. None matches the Maxwellian collisionless whole-space target.
- **Voltage–conductance global weak existence:** [2607.22112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22112) establishes a new existence theorem. The retained problem asks for genuinely time-periodic solutions of the full PDE, not global weak existence.
- **NNLIF oscillations in reduced dynamics:** [Ikeda–Roux–Salort–Smets](https://mna.episciences.org/10212) constructs periodic solutions for a reduced delay equation. The full finite-delay Fokker–Planck target is retained and explicitly distinguished.
- **Time-periodic slowest-diffuser selection:** temporal heterogeneity changes the classical Dockery mechanism. The retained N-species question uses a static heterogeneous resource and distinct constant diffusion rates.
- **Lane–Emden stable or symmetric subcases:** [2510.06613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06613) proves a stricter parameter region; [2608.10501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10501) concerns a different stable-solution assertion. The unrestricted higher-dimensional subcritical conjecture remains the target.
- **Local kinetic Krylov–Safonov:** [2609.15534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15534) addresses local second-order diffusion. Retained question is fractional kinetic diffusion with only evenness in the increment and two-sided kernel bounds.
- **Commuting nonlinear viscosity matrices:** [2512.15620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15620), revised May 2026, assumes the diffusion matrix commutes with the flux Jacobian. The retained vanishing-viscosity problem removes that structural hypothesis.
- **Glimm–Lax uniqueness:** [Cheng–Faile–Krupa, ARMA 250 (2026), article 82](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-026-02232-1) proves uniqueness under positive fractional regularity; its §1.6 still asks for all small measurable data. Only the latter is retained.
- **One-dimensional nonuniqueness under Liu admissibility:** [2403.07784](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07784), published 2026, does not impose the strictly convex entropy inequality in the retained Bressan question.
- **Inhomogeneous two-dimensional Navier–Stokes weak uniqueness:** [2406.19907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19907) announces the matching bounded-positive-density theorem. Excluded.
- **Monotone Prandtl questions:** the stationary result [10.1016/j.anihpc.2021.02.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anihpc.2021.02.007) and the dynamical manuscript [2606.13134](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13134) remove or claim to remove the candidate barriers. Excluded.
- **Elastic Lavrentiev gap for continuous deformations:** [Almi–Krömer–Molchanova](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00033-023-02132-4) provides a counterexample. Excluded.
- **Neo-Hookean regularity of unrelaxed weak-closure minimizers:** [2603.22873](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22873), Theorem 1.1, proves a gap. It is not an answer to attainment of the infimum restricted to the regular admissible class. That distinction is recorded in the retained SIAM-based entry.
- **Strong-KPP uniqueness on bounded Lipschitz domains:** [Berestycki–Graham 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-026-02168-6), Theorem 7.1, resolves the earlier bounded-domain conjecture. Retained targets concern general unbounded domains, critical extinction, and non-KPP reactions outside two obstacles; none is a renamed bounded-domain question.
- **Bilinear gelation:** [1902.07686](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.07686) proves continuity for kernels bilinear in additive conserved types. The retained general strongly-gelling class does not assume that representation.
- **Willmore fixed-isoperimetric-ratio existence at higher genus:** [ARMA 250 (2026), article 81](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-026-02235-y) gives new existence results. Excluded to avoid an outdated candidate.

Deduplication also checked the 352 admitted titles and neighboring agents' reservations. In particular, this batch does not add another Euler/Navier–Stokes regularity problem, another inhomogeneous Coulomb Landau global-existence problem, or another weak Boltzmann uniqueness problem. The two Zakharov problems ask finite-time collapse versus low-energy scattering; the two FENE problems ask smoothness versus a singular diffusion limit; the two Landau problems ask ancient rigidity versus uniform macroscopic smoothing. These are different targets, not parameter subdivisions.
