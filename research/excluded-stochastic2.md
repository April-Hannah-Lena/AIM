# Stochastic candidates excluded or narrowed in the expansion

Status screening date: **8 September 2026**. These notes are outside the 200 active entries.

| Candidate | Screening result and action |
| --- | --- |
| A discontinuous transition in one-dimensional pair/triplet creation with fast stirring | Durrett, [*Discontinuous phase transitions in the one-dimensional pair and triplet creation processes* (30 August 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29878), §2.3, claims a rigorous proof for both models. Its microscopic generator matches the proposed entry. The September book manuscript still lists the question; the newer specific theorem takes precedence. Removed the draft of 191; this record does not independently certify the preprint proof. |
| Analyticity of biased-walk speed on a Galton–Watson tree without leaves | The updated Lyons–Peres book marks the old question as resolved by Bowditch–Tokushige, [*The speed of a biased random walk on a Galton–Watson tree is analytic* (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.03433). Excluded; 180 asks monotonicity over the full positive-speed interval. |
| Equivalence of Sznitman's quantitative ballisticity conditions | Guerra–Ramírez, [*A proof of Sznitman's conjecture about ballistic RWRE* (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.02011), establish the equivalence. Excluded; 185 asks the separate implication from directional transience. |
| Density conjecture for activated random walk on the line | Hoffman–Johnson–Junge [2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01731), with Forien's [2025 proof](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02579), resolve the one-dimensional question. Only the two-dimensional square-box case is included as 187. |
| Nonexistence of bigeodesics in exactly solvable directed last-passage models | Basu–Hoffman–Sly, [*Nonexistence of Bigeodesics in Integrable Models of Last Passage Percolation* (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-021-04246-0), treat these models. Entry 178 instead concerns unrestricted undirected minimum passage in the full plane. |
| Strong disorder versus very strong disorder for standard bulk directed polymers | Junk–Lacoin, [*Strong disorder and very strong disorder are equivalent for directed polymers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02562), and the later developments recorded in Berger's 2026 notes settle the corresponding long-standing bulk question under their hypotheses. Entry 198 is renewal pinning, whose analogue is explicitly still conjectured. |
| Hard-disk percolation at arbitrarily small positive enlargement | Magazinov, [*On percolation of two-dimensional hard disks*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.03111), proves high-activity percolation for every positive enlargement. Excluded; 194 asks Gibbs nonuniqueness, not geometric percolation. |
| A general claim that no stochastic-heat uniqueness theorem exists at or below Hölder exponent 3/4 | Han's [August 2026 theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01279) gives uniqueness in law under nondegeneracy above exponent 2/3. Such a broad claim would be outdated. Entry 197 permits vanishing coefficients and specifically asks pathwise uniqueness at 3/4. |

The partial strict-convexity theorem for FPP, half-plane Richardson exclusion, and numerical observations of ecological coexistence or a fluid transition were inspected as potential resolutions. Their hypotheses or conclusions do not match the retained entries; the distinctions are explained on the individual pages.
