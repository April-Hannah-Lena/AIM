# Exclusions considered for problems 096–120

Checked 2026-09-08. A recent preprint claiming a full resolution is sufficient reason to omit a candidate from this open-problem collection. Listing that claim here does not certify its proof or imply that it has been refereed. Narrower results were compared against the exact hypotheses of retained entries.

- **Existence of a bound state in every convex magnetic sector.** Kachmar and Sundqvist, [Bound states for the magnetic Neumann Laplacian in planar sectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12600), submitted 14 July 2026, Theorem 1.1, claims the full strict half-plane-threshold inequality. The earlier draft of 111 was removed. Entry 106 instead records the older, distinct strict ordering of energies for two different convex opening angles, which this theorem does not establish.
- **A magnetic Neumann disk energy below the de Gennes threshold at every field.** Léna and Sundqvist, [A magnetic eigenvalue bound in the disk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24188), submitted 22 May 2026, claims the full bound. This cannot serve as an open replacement for the sector existence question.
- **Planar Pompeiu and Schiffer conjectures.** [A computer-assisted counterexample to planar Pompeiu and Schiffer conjectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01579) and [Counterexamples to Schiffer’s Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05114), August 2026, claim counterexamples. Omitted while those recent claims are assessed.
- **Yau’s first-eigenvalue conjecture for embedded minimal hypersurfaces of the sphere.** Lingzhong Zeng, [A proof of Yau’s conjecture on the first eigenvalue of embedded minimal hypersurfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06123), 2025, claims a full proof, while later partial-result literature still discusses an open conjecture. This ambiguous case was omitted; it is distinct from the free-boundary Steklov problem in 116.
- **Generic nondegeneracy of periodic spectral edges.** Zhongkai Tao and Mengxuan Yang, [arXiv:2608.30189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30189), 31 August 2026, claims a generic-edge theorem. Omitted; entries 096–098 concern different spectral properties.
- **Ashbaugh–Benguria’s reciprocal Neumann-eigenvalue inequality.** Yixin He, Yanyang Li and Quanyu Tang, [arXiv:2606.08271](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08271), June 2026, claims a full solution.
- **The reciprocal Dirichlet eigenvalue-gap conjecture.** Yanyang Li, Quanyu Tang and Haiqi Zhang, [arXiv:2607.01135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01135), July 2026, claims a proof. Omitted rather than reproducing the older open status.
- **The Steklov shrinking-volume/fixed-diameter question.** Pingxin Gu, [arXiv:2608.30807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30807), 31 August 2026, gives new isodiametric inequalities and claims a solution of the relevant survey question. This is not the fixed embedded boundary question in 119.
- **Convexity of Coulomb ground-state energy for unrestricted external potentials.** Di Marino, Lewin and Nenna, [Ground state energy is not always convex in the number of electrons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08632), 2024, give a counterexample involving six fractional nuclear charges. [Martínez González and Ayers (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpclett.5c03648) discuss further Coulomb model counterexamples. Entry 102 deliberately retains the single integer-charge atom problem.
- **Dry Ten Martini outside critical coupling.** Avila, You and Zhou, [arXiv:2306.16254](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16254), prove the noncritical assertion. For critical coupling, [Borgnia and Slager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06869) make a resolution claim, while [Cedzich and Li](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06710) discuss a remaining critical problem. The critical candidate was omitted because the conflicting claims require a deeper audit.

No excluded problem was counted toward the 25 new entries. Spectral computations appearing as evidence in source papers do not make the retained statements numerical linear algebra problems: the requested conclusions are theorems about differential operators, geometry, or quantum dynamics.
