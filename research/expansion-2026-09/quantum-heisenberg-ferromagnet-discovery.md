## Proposed family and formulation checkpoint

The proposed family asks for positive-temperature long-range magnetic order in the nearest-neighbour isotropic quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet on a cubic lattice of dimension at least three. A 2025 specialist formulation now supplies the normalized, volume-averaged spin-correlation observable explicitly.

Before any public draft, retain this provisional fingerprint: a fixed dimension d at least three; a fixed positive half-integer spin S; finite lattice boxes; the tensor product of the spin-S spaces; the Hamiltonian summing S squared minus the spin dot product over unordered nearest-neighbour bonds; and the zero-field finite-volume Gibbs trace at positive temperature T. The target is positivity of the thermodynamic limit of the sum of all two-point dot-product correlations divided by the square of the number of sites, for every sufficiently small positive T. The volume limit precedes the low-temperature limit. Boundary conventions and whether to retain the full limit or a sourced liminf need final checking before drafting. Different spins and dimensions remain one family.

The finite-volume signed magnetization vanishes by symmetry, so that signed mean is not the requested order parameter. Ground-state order does not settle the positive-temperature question. The fixed-spin quantum problem must not be replaced by classical vector spins, a large-spin limit, an antiferromagnet or a mean-field graph.

Full nearest pages 201, 210 and 307 were read. Entry 196 concerns the spin-one antiferromagnetic chain's spectral gap; entry 205 concerns two-dimensional antiferromagnetic ground-state staggered order; entry 299 concerns disordered classical Ising-spin replica overlap. Those models and observables differ. Local aliases were searched across active problems, candidate records and exclusions. A final upstream duplicate refresh and a separated adversarial review have not been completed for admission.

- **S25:** Robert Seiringer, *The Heisenberg ferromagnet: a dilute Bose gas in disguise*, in *Mini-Workshop: New Directions in Correlated Quantum Systems*, Oberwolfach Reports 22 (2025), no. 1, pp.357–358; [publisher report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/51350), DOI 10.4171/OWR/2025/7. The entire contribution and its two references were read in the publisher PDF text, pp.357–358 (PDF pages 19–20). It explicitly states the thermal order problem and its averaged correlation formula. Its free-energy discussion is separate. Its two-dimensional free-energy open-status sentence predates K26 below. S25 shares an author with CGS15 and NS21; those are not independent teams.
- **CGS15:** Michele Correggi, Alessandro Giuliani and Robert Seiringer, [*Validity of the spin-wave approximation for the free energy of the Heisenberg ferromagnet*](https://arxiv.org/html/1312.7873v2), Communications in Mathematical Physics 339 (2015), 279–307, DOI 10.1007/s00220-015-2402-0. The introduction, model, complete Theorem 2.1, equations (2.6)–(2.8), their discussion and the opening technical results were read. The inspected arXiv history has v1 December 30, 2013 and latest v2 August 10, 2019. The theorem establishes the leading three-dimensional free energy for fixed spin. The correlation bound controls distances growing with inverse temperature; it does not establish infinite-distance order at a fixed positive temperature. Appendix B's full proof has not been read and the proof is not independently certified.
- **NS21:** Marcin Napiórkowski and Robert Seiringer, [*Free energy asymptotics of the quantum Heisenberg spin chain*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11005-021-01375-4), Letters in Mathematical Physics 111 (2021), article 31, published March 9, 2021, DOI 10.1007/s11005-021-01375-4. Introduction, model, full Theorem 2.1 and the opening boson representation were read. The introduction explicitly retains low-temperature order in dimensions at least three as unresolved. The theorem concerns one-dimensional free energy, not three-dimensional thermal correlations. Its older two-dimensional lower-bound question must be updated using K26.
- **U13:** Daniel Ueltschi, [*Random loop representations for quantum spin systems*](https://arxiv.org/html/1301.0811v3), Journal of Mathematical Physics 54 (2013), 083301, DOI 10.1063/1.4817865. Introduction, opening loop definitions, full Theorem 5.1 and the ferromagnetic model in section 7.1 were read. The latest inspected arXiv version is v3, July 9, 2013. Theorem 5.1 restricts the crossing parameter to the interval from zero to one-half. Section 7.1 identifies the spin-one-half Heisenberg ferromagnet with parameter one and loop weight two. Substituting that excluded parameter into the theorem would be invalid. Complete correlation identities and the standing periodic-box conventions need further reading if used in a public formulation. Higher-spin biquadratic theorems were not fully audited.
- **BFU20:** Jakob E. Björnberg, Jürg Fröhlich and Daniel Ueltschi, [*Quantum Spins and Random Loops on the Complete Graph*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-019-03634-x), Communications in Mathematical Physics 375 (2020), 1629–1663, online November 27, 2019, DOI 10.1007/s00220-019-03634-x. Introduction, section 2's spin space, Hamiltonian (2.3), Gibbs state (2.4), magnetization definitions (2.10)–(2.17) and complete Theorem 2.1 were read. All pairs interact with coupling proportional to the reciprocal of vertex count. The theorem gives the limiting magnetization generating function on that complete graph, not the nearest-neighbour cubic lattice. Section 1 explicitly distinguishes its lattice heuristics from its rigorous complete-graph calculation. This author team overlaps U13.
- **F02:** Paul Federbush, [*For the Quantum Heisenberg Ferromagnet, Tao to the Proof of a Phase Transition*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0202044), arXiv:math-ph/0202044, February 27, 2002, preprint. The full eleven-page manuscript was read. Section 2 gives a conditional criterion; section 3's central approximation is conjectural, and section 6 says it still needs proof. The title is therefore not evidence of a completed phase-transition proof. The paper's spin-one-half Hamiltonian is twice the shifted nearest-neighbour normalization proposed above. The full version history still needs a final check.
- **K26:** Andreas Klippel, [*Free-Energy Asymptotics of the Two-Dimensional Quantum Heisenberg Ferromagnet*](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25506v1), arXiv:2608.25506v1, August 26, 2026, preprint. The only version in the inspected history is v1. Introduction, free-boundary model (1.1), thermodynamic limit (1.2) and complete Theorem 1.1 were read. The result concerns the leading free energy in dimension two. The paragraph immediately following equation (1.6) explicitly disclaims an assertion of magnetic order. The technical proof was not independently certified. The bibliography led to S25.
