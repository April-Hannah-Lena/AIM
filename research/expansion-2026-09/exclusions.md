## Gaussian multiple-access feedback capacity — held claim

The full region for three or more senders is explicitly open in later specialist sources, but Wu–Vishwanath–Arapostathis (Allerton 2005), introduction and §IV-C, makes a matching broad capacity claim. Its actual argument was read; no explicit independent adjudication was located. Held outside the count. Equal-power sum capacity is already solved, and 2024 identification capacity is a different decoding task. See [candidate record](candidates/gaussian-mac-feedback.json).
