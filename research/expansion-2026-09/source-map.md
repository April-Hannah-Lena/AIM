# Source map

This is a discovery and reading log, not a certificate of open status. Actual access date: 2026-09-17.

## Sources inspected

- **Communication complexity:** Hambardzumyan–Lovett–Shirley, [The Log-Rank Conjecture: New Equivalent Formulations](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.02583v3), v3 (2026-08-14), §§1–3; Sudakov–Tomon, [Matrix discrepancy and the log-rank conjecture](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~sudakovb/discrepancy-logrank-conjecture.pdf), Mathematical Programming 212 (2025), 567–579, §1 and Theorem 1.1. Further lower-bound and apparent-refutation checks are detailed in [the accepted record](candidates/log-rank.json).
- **Finite-state control:** Volkov, [reset-threshold survey](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.15655v4), v4 (January 13, 2026), §§1.1–1.2 and footnote 1; Szykuła, [Synchronizing Automata: Open Problems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24245v1), EPTCS 451 (2026), §§1–2.6 and Conjecture 1. Zhu's July 2026 one-cluster theorem and earlier full-proof claims were compared in [the accepted record](candidates/cerny.json).
- **Statistical inference:** Brennan–Bresler–Huleihel, PMLR 75 (2018), Conjecture 1; Huleihel–Mazumdar–Pal, JMLR 25 (2024), Conjecture 4; independent Hirahara–Shimizu, ECCC TR24-058, and Avrachenkov et al., arXiv:2510.12365v2 (April 2026). Detection risk, vanishing edge-counting advantage and geometric-graph scope are distinguished in [the accepted record](candidates/planted-clique.json).
- **Approximation hardness:** Fei–Minzer–Wang, [ECCC TR26-179](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/179/download/), September 14, 2026, Conjecture 1.4 and Theorem 1.6; Kempe–Regev–Toner, arXiv:0710.0655v3; Heilman, arXiv:2608.00333v1, Theorem 1.4. Classical/entangled and one-to-one/four-to-one distinctions, recent hypergraph consequences and vertex-cover proof claims are documented in [the accepted record](candidates/unique-games.json).
- **Gaussian geometry:** Isaksson–Mossel, arXiv:0903.3362v3, Conjecture 1.4; independent Heilman–Tarter, [Forum of Mathematics, Sigma 9 (2021), e65](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2021.56), Conjecture 1.6. Conditional hyperstability, three-class progress, unequal-mass counterexamples, and weak-simplex coding results are compared in [the accepted record](candidates/gaussian-simplex.json).
- **Variational elasticity:** Pedregal, [arXiv:1905.06571v5](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.06571v5), June 18, 2026, Theorems 1.1–1.2, claims the exact planar implication. The earlier withdrawn version does not adjudicate the revised argument; [held record](candidates/morrey-planar.json).
- **Mathematical biology:** Wang, Othmer and Maini, [Problems, Progress and Perspectives in Mathematical and Computational Biology](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/maini/PKM%20publications/559.pdf), Bulletin of Mathematical Biology 88:74 (2026), DOI 10.1007/s11538-026-01620-8. All five editorial pages read. The joint BMB/JMB collection has 17 contributions; individual mathematical statements still require full-text investigation. Routes include motif-avoidant attractors in asynchronous Boolean networks (Pastva et al., 2025), planar chemical reaction limit cycles (Craciun–Erban, 2025), phenotype-structured migration (Lorenzi–Painter–Villa, 2025), and discretely structured growth models (Walker–Byrne, 2025). These are discovery leads, not accepted entries.
