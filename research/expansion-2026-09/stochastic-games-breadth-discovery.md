# Breadth discovery preceding the simple-stochastic-games draft

Checked September 19, 2026. These are discovery notes, not accepted entries or additional formal candidate records. Actual queries S531–S532 and S533-4 are in the search log.

- **Unknot recognition:** Chad Musick, *Locally Minimal Bridge Presentations of Knots*, [arXiv:2609.06492](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06492), v1 September 6, 2026, claims polynomial-time recognition in its abstract. The metadata and single-version history were read, but the full claimed theorem and proof were not adjudicated. This prevents treating the old question as an accepted open lead; neither correctness nor a proof error is asserted.
- **Asynchronous Boolean-network biology:** Samuel Pastva and coauthors, *An open problem: Why are motif-avoidant attractors so rare in asynchronous Boolean networks?*, Journal of Mathematical Biology 91 (2025), article 11, [publisher full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00285-025-02235-8), published June 12, 2025. The introduction, model definitions, relevant examples, §7.4 and discussion were read. The article studies terminal strongly connected components outside minimal trap spaces, supplies counterexamples to natural structural guesses and gives delay bounds. A precise independently corroborated remaining theorem-level target has not yet been selected. Empirical rarity is not a new universal conjecture.
- **Memory in stochastic games:** search surfaced Hansen, Ibsen-Jensen and Neyman's *The Big Match with a Clock and a Bit of Memory*, Mathematics of Operations Research 48(1) (2023), 419–432, [author manuscript](https://livrepository.liverpool.ac.uk/3029558/1/finiteBM.pdf), and their [2025 limited-public-memory preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02623). The general private-memory question needs full-text quantifier and current-status review. These are the same author team, not independent corroboration. A finite-memory result for the Big Match itself must not be repackaged as an open question. This simultaneous/long-run-payoff setting must also be distinguished from finite turn-based reachability games with positional optimality.
- **Other searches:** densest-subgraph approximation, constant-colour algorithms, queueing stability and square-peg leads were considered for discovery only. No complete formulation/status audit was finished. The queueing search surfaced a Gamarnik–Meyn counterexample to an exponential-tail inference from fluid stability, so the historical assertion must not be reused without checking that result.

The simple-stochastic-games candidate followed a separate full source, scope, duplicate and adversarial review; see its [evidence record](candidates/simple-stochastic-games-polynomial-time.json). AI-generated search aggregations were discovery routes only. Primary papers supplied the admitted statement and status evidence.
