## Entry 286: matching k-server claim

**Checked:** September 17, 2026. **Disposition:** matching resolution claim; complete proof and independent assessment not reviewed.

Christian Coester, Elias Koutsoupias and Marek Zbysiński, *The k-server conjecture is true*, [arXiv:2609.15979v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15979v1), submitted September 14, 2026. The primary metadata, abstract, §1.1 model and full Theorem 1.2 were read. The theorem claims deterministic work-function competitiveness on every metric space, with additive constant equal to the sum of pairwise distances in the initial configuration. This directly covers the finite-metric formulation of [entry 286](../../problems/286-deterministic-k-server.md), including a sequence-independent constant and the same initial placement for the offline optimum.

The claim postdates entry 286's September 13 review. Searches S213-4 and B286-1 located the primary manuscript; its version history still showed only v1, with no correction or withdrawal notice located. That observation does not certify the proof. The historical page and its ID have not been silently changed or retired, and this follow-up contributes zero additions. A subsequent status decision should inspect the full argument and available specialist assessments rather than infer validity from the title.

## Entries 031–032: terminal-discrepancy resolution claims

**Checked:** September 17, 2026. **Disposition:** matching claims; complete technical proofs not independently certified here.

Shengtao Guo, Ethan X. Fang and Junwei Lu, *Vector Balancing via Directional Total Variation*, [arXiv:2609.11189v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11189v1), September 10, 2026, Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2, claim the universal terminal-vector bound and its sparse-incidence consequence. The full statements, the corollary's scaling proof and §1.2 scope discussion were read. They match [031](../../problems/031-komlos.md) and [032](../../problems/032-beck-fiala.md), respectively, across all stated dimensions and sparsity regimes.

Sankeerth Rao Karingula and Shachar Lovett, *An elementary proof of the Komlos conjecture*, [ECCC TR26-188](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/188/), September 17, 2026, independently acknowledge the first manuscript and present another proof with terminal constant 36. Its full Theorem 1.2, Lemmas 1.4–1.5 and the reduction proving Theorem 1.2 were read. This is an independently authored follow-up, not an independent proof certification by this research process. The reports' complete technical arguments and subsequent specialist assessments require review before a catalogue retirement decision.

Both manuscripts distinguish terminal control from simultaneous control of all prefixes. The latter has its own [accepted companion draft](drafts/strong-komlos-prefix-discrepancy.md); it does not replace or reuse either historical ID. These claims postdate the original September 8 reviews. The original pages remain preserved and these correction leads contribute zero additions.
