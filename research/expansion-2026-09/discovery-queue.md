## Queueing and polyhedral optimization (S136–S142; 2026-09-17)

- Polynomial Hirsch passed the separated A23 self-pass and moved to an [accepted research draft record](candidates/polynomial-hirsch.json). It concerns undirected edge-path existence, distinct from the efficient monotone simplex rule in entry 100. The recent circuit-walk theorem and coherent/monotone-path counterexamples were compared at theorem level. It has no active ID yet.
- Slow-server threshold policies remain discovery-only. Ger Koole, *The slow-server problem with multiple slow servers*, Queueing Systems 100 (2022), 469–471, [DOI 10.1007/s11134-022-09761-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11134-022-09761-y), §2, states the three-server question with a general Markovian arrival stream, but the displayed state omits the arrival phase. The [author-uploaded text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/360423865_The_slow-server_problem_with_multiple_slow_servers), §§1–3, was read. Wang–Ameen–Chen–Hong–Nichols–Zurek's 2026 [author manuscript](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~weinaw/pdf/One_fast_two_slow_conjecture.pdf), §§1–3.3 and full Theorem 1, proves the Poisson-arrival case with one fast and two identical slow servers, for discounted and stable average system-population cost. A larger identical-slow-server family or a phase-dependent Markov-arrival conjecture still needs an exact source-backed formulation and independent status evidence. Do not invent that extension from the title.
- Jali–Qu–Wang–Joshi, *Efficient Reinforcement Learning for Routing Jobs in Heterogeneous Queueing Systems*, [AISTATS 2024 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/jali24a/jali24a.pdf), §§1–3.3, uses a finite buffer and server-specific thresholds; its general learning guarantee does not establish global threshold-policy optimality. Koole §3 discusses incomplete older Rykov and Luh–Viniotis arguments. De Véricourt–Zhou's 2006 critique was located at abstract level, not read in full. These distinctions prevent admission at present. The primary arXiv record for 2607.22580 displays a June 8 submission date inconsistent with the identifier and indexed July dates; no precise posting date is asserted here.
- The asymmetric metric TSP subtour integrality-gap lead passed A24 and is an [accepted research draft](candidates/asymmetric-tsp-integrality-gap.json). The separately sourced asymmetric factor-two problem differs from symmetric entry 285. Full LP-equivalence, constant-factor, finite-computation and random-instance theorem comparisons are recorded. The current general upper bound is $12+2\sqrt{2}$ from Vygen’s March 2026 preprint; the June paper’s quoted 17 is superseded. Traub–Vygen’s book was published in 2025 (author PDF copyright 2024). No active ID yet.
