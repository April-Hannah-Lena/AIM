## Gaussian statistics follow-up (S182–S187 and A29; 2026-09-17)

The polynomial U-conjecture passed full formulation, current-source, restricted-result and duplicate checks and the separated A29 self-pass. It has moved to an [accepted research draft](candidates/gaussian-polynomial-unlinking.json), without an active ID. Lan–Ouimet–Sun's August 27, 2026 revision explicitly retains the standard-Gaussian unlinking formulation. The strong-GPI claim was inspected at full Theorem 2.1 and opening-proof level: it concerns powers of jointly Gaussian coordinates, not arbitrary polynomial statistics. Its broader correctness remains unadjudicated here. Long's Gaussian-moments Theorem 5.1 uses complex coefficients and a different moment condition; it does not refute real-polynomial unlinking.

The heat-flow entropy derivative lead remains outside the accepted count. S188–S189 found Yuzhou Gu and Mark Sellke, *A Counterexample to the Gaussian Completely Monotone Conjecture*, [arXiv:2605.11656v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.11656v1), May 12, 2026. The full introduction, Theorems 1–2 and explicit finite-support measure were read. Their convention is $H=\int f\log f$ and heat generator $\partial_x^2$: Theorem 1 asserts a positive fifth derivative of $H$, violating the proposed alternating signs; Theorem 2 also claims failure for log-concave inputs at some order. With Shannon entropy $h=-H$, the fifth derivative has the opposite sign, so there is no convention-based escape. A positive-time shift gives smooth Gaussian-mixture initial data (inference from the semigroup law). This is a matching claim, not the previously excluded Boltzmann/Kac question. The complete proof and verification code have not been independently adjudicated here. Do not admit this lead from Fan Cheng's older conference abstract. A multidimensional companion, [arXiv:2605.18081](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18081), was located at primary abstract level; its full theorem was not reviewed.

These leads have not passed the candidate-record requirements and do not increase any accepted or integrated count. Checked/discovered on 2026-09-17. Search IDs refer to `search-log.json`.
