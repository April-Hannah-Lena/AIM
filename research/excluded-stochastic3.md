# Candidates excluded or narrowed: stochastic dynamics and optimization

Screening date: **13 September 2026**. These notes are outside the 300-entry catalogue.

| Candidate | Reason for exclusion or narrower formulation |
| --- | --- |
| The 5/6-density conjecture for pinwheel scheduling | Kawamura, [*A proof of the 5/6-density conjecture for pinwheel scheduling* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2530214123), supplies a recent proof. This candidate was removed. |
| Stability of the greedy server on a circle | Rolla–Sidoravicius, [*Stability of the Greedy Algorithm on the Circle*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.2389), establishes the old queueing conjecture. Not included. |
| Uniform constant comparison for completely general Gibbs scans | Gaitonde–Mossel, [arXiv:2410.11136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11136), contains obstructions to stronger general comparisons. Entry 270 specifically retains the ferromagnetic Ising question in Levin–Peres. |
| Constant approximation for restricted Santa Claus allocation | This restricted valuation model already has constant approximations. Entry 282 permits arbitrary agent-item values, as in [Bamas et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08453). |
| A proof of the entire planar Steiner-ratio conjecture inferred from historical claims | The [September 2026 primary revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22365v3) still seeks the full inequality and gives a lower bound below it. Entry 279 discloses the historical status issue and cites the restricted five-point theorem accurately. |
| Existence of sandpile avalanche exponents in dimensions at least five | [Hutchcroft’s high-dimensional theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.04120) treats these dimensions. Entry 284 is planar and counts total topplings rather than avalanche area. |
| Axelrod clustering with two possible traits per feature | [Lanchier–Schweinsberg](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.4413) prove the two-state theorem. Entry 286 requires at least three traits. |
| Mean cluster spacing in the bounded-confidence model counted as the strong 2R conjecture | [Dey–Etesami–Gopalan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08299) distinguish the expectation result from their strong conjecture. Entry 287 asks convergence of the cluster-location process. |
| Linear alignment variance for sufficiently biased binary strings | [Lember–Matzinger’s theorem](https://math.ut.ee/sites/default/files/inline-files/08-aop436.pdf) covers biased strings. Entry 289 uses independent fair bits. |
| Gaussian lattice-polymer universality inferred from weak-noise theorems or numerical transfer matrices | [Agarwal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21453) treats intermediate disorder; the [2026 transfer-matrix study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14477) is numerical. Neither establishes entry 290 with fixed unit noise. |
| Polynomial mixing for three-state critical Potts dynamics | [Gheissari–Lubetzky](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.02182) prove that case. Entry 291 asks the four-state endpoint, for which their bound is quasi-polynomial. |

The first 200 entries were checked for duplicate topics before selection. Existing crystallization, percolation, ecological coexistence, and polymer questions were not counted again. Entries 280 and 289 concern different observables (mean coefficient and variance order) in one alignment model; 281 and 282 concern different optimization objectives with a known reduction between them. These relationships are disclosed rather than represented as logical independence.
