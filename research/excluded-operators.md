# Operator and numerical problems excluded during the 2026-09-08 review

These candidates were checked against current primary literature and were not counted among the 100 open entries. Several results below are recent preprints. Recording their announced resolution is a reason to exclude an old open formulation, not a claim that this repository independently refereed the proofs.

| Candidate | Evidence found | Editorial decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scalar Crouzeix conjecture, constant 2 | E. Lorist and F. Schwenninger, [A solution to Crouzeix’s conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03841), August 2026. A. Townsend and A. Greenbaum’s [expert account](https://alextownsend.net/essays/SIAMNews_CrouzeixConjecture.pdf), dated 15 August, also reports checking Teng Jin’s proof with Michel Crouzeix. | Excluded the scalar statement. Entry 026 concerns the distinct complete, matrix-valued version, explicitly retained beyond order three in [Åhag–Czyż–Virtanen, September 2026 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27346). |
| Heil–Ramanathan–Topiwala conjecture for arbitrary nonzero square-integrable functions | M. Faulhuber, P. Petersen, J. T. van Velthoven and F. Voigtlaender, [Linear dependence of time-frequency shifts of a Schwartz function](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05044), August 2026. The paper gives a twelve-shift counterexample; its second version adds an analytic proof alongside the certified explicit example. | Excluded the universal assertion. |
| Forsythe’s universal convergence conjecture for restarted conjugate gradients | M. J. Colbrook, G. Stepaniants and A. Townsend, [A Complete Resolution of Forsythe’s Conjecture for Restarted Conjugate Gradients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04659), submitted 4 September 2026. The abstract reports validity precisely for restart lengths 1, 2 and 3, and counterexamples for each larger length. | Excluded rather than repeating the February 2026 workshop’s obsolete open status. |
| Polynomial worst-case growth for Gaussian elimination with complete pivoting | R. Shah and J. Urschel, [Entry growth in Gaussian elimination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19189), August 2026, revised 31 August. The paper identifies quasipolynomial worst-case growth for complete and rook pivoting. | Excluded the polynomial-growth conjecture and the old unresolved growth-order question. |
| Polynomial conditioning of submatrices selected by QR with column pivoting under orthogonality/incoherence | L. Chen, Z. Liu, W. He and B. Dong, [Iteris: Agentic Research Loops for Computational Mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02484), June 2026. Its second case study supplies a counterexample even with low coherence. | Excluded this QR-selection conjecture. The same paper’s restricted CG-versus-coordinate-descent comparison is not a solution to every comparison problem. |
| Two-copy undistillability of Werner states in the one-copy-undistillable parameter region | T. C. Fraser, F. Huber, B. Pozsgay and I. Vona, [On the two-copy distillability of Werner states and a new partial trace inequality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24309), July 2026. | Excluded the two-copy question. Entry 037 requires undistillability for every number of copies, a stronger unresolved condition. |
| Permanent-on-top and Bapat–Sunder conjectures | F. Zhang, [An update on a few permanent conjectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.02844), 2016, reviews counterexamples and separates them from Lieb and Chollet. | Excluded these false stronger assertions; entries 027–028 have their own statements and checks. |

Searches included the exact candidate names together with `2026`, `proof`, `counterexample`, `solution` and `open`, followed by examination of the primary records above. The searches also found announced progress on Schatten Hanner inequalities; that candidate was withheld because relying on an old open range without resolving the announcement would be misleading.
