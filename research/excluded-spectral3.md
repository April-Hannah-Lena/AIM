# Exclusions and proof-claim checks for problems 201–225

Checked on 2026-09-13. These candidates or superficially matching claims were inspected while selecting the batch. A recent preprint proof claim is sufficient reason to avoid presenting its exact target as safely open; the project does not independently certify every proof.

| Candidate or search hit | Evidence and disposition |
|---|---|
| Landau–Pekar effective mass of the strong-coupling Fröhlich polaron | [Morris Brooks (13 September 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08835), abstract and main theorem, supplies the sharp lower bound completing the effective-mass asymptotic. Excluded. |
| Brouwer’s bound on sums of the largest graph-Laplacian eigenvalues | [Pravesh Kothari and Stefan Tudose (10 June 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12197) explicitly gives a proof of the full conjecture. Excluded as a matching recent proof claim. |
| Absence of curved-waveguide bound states in sufficiently strong magnetic fields | [Enguerrand Bon-Lavigne, Loïc Le Treust, Nicolas Raymond, and Julien Royer (2022; published 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12733), main theorem, constructs persistent discrete spectrum contrary to the Duclos–Exner prediction. Excluded. |
| No-low-energy-trivial-states conjecture | [Anurag Anshu, Nikolas Breuckmann, and Chinmay Nirkhe (2022, revised 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13228) proves NLTS. Excluded; quantum PCP in207 remains a different assertion. |
| Wu correction in the Gross–Pitaevskii scaling | [Cristina Caraci (2026 review)](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0310157) reviews the established Gross–Pitaevskii correction. Excluded in that scaling;204 concerns the fixed-potential thermodynamic dilute limit. |
| Apparent full Fourier-restriction proof in an outdated search title | [Eric Sawyer, current version and history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18457v8) records a withdrawal on 2 January 2025. The February 2026 version studies testing-condition comparisons. The old headline is not a resolution;215 retained. |
| Full-range boson-star uniqueness inferred from an August 2026 paper | [Pan Chen and Xiaoyu Zeng (9 August 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08493), introduction and Theorem1.1, still distinguish small mass from the whole subcritical interval. The small-mass question was excluded;208 retains the full interval. |
| Exact Hubbard order inferred from Hirsch mean-field gap asymptotics | [Edwin Langmann and Jonatan Lenells (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11005-026-02085-5) resolve a mean-field asymptotic. It does not resolve the interacting ground-state order in211. |
| Full indicator-disk damping result inferred from superellipse decay | [Achammer and Kleinhenz (4 June 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06166), Theorem1.1, requires positive-order smooth vanishing of damping. The discontinuous indicator in222 remains outside its hypotheses. |

Existing spectral problems001–025 and101–125 were also checked for overlap. In particular, eigenfunction random-wave behavior, quantum unique ergodicity, Anderson localization/delocalization, ionization bounds, and earlier spectral shape inequalities were not duplicated. BGS level spacings, Berry–Tabor nearest-neighbor statistics, quantum-information capacity, and many-body gap questions have different target objects and conclusions.
