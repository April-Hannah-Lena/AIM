## Withdrawn claim: do not classify as a solved exclusion

Search snippets for Spyros Alexakis and Matti Lassas, *Lens rigidity in 2D: The reconstruction of a Riemann surface from its geodesic lengths* (October 2025), announce broad lens rigidity. The [current arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27085) instead records withdrawal on 6 January 2026: the authors found a sign error affecting the later proof. No entry in this collection relies on that claim. The unrestricted two-dimensional lens question was left out of this batch; its omission does **not** mean it has been solved.

## Further distinctions preserved in the problem files

- Photoacoustic inversion with a known variable sound speed does not resolve joint source/speed recovery from one experiment (064).
- Sufficiently-large-time null control and small-time control to nonnegative states do not settle small-time null control in 071.
- Local billiard integrability near an ellipse does not prove the global Birkhoff conjecture (069).
- Nonzero-frequency conductivity counterexamples in [Daudé–Helffer–Kamran–Nicoleau (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14063) do not answer the zero-frequency scalar conductivity question in 051.

The individual files contain the open assertion, the search phrases used in the review, and the limitations of the located partial results. The review checks the public literature available to the search tools; it does not certify that no unpublished, unindexed or overlooked solution exists.
