# Exclusions and status distinctions for problems 221–244

Checked on **2026-09-13**. The first group records questions omitted after locating a resolution or a recent reported resolution. The second records nearby results that were checked but do not settle a retained problem. A preprint is described as a preprint; inclusion here is not an independent verification of its proof.

## Results checked against retained statements

| Retained entry | Nearby result and why the target differs |
| --- | --- |
| 221: fixed unit ABC fast dynamo | Sorella–Villringer, [*A limsup fast dynamo on T³*](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.23024v2), §§2–3: a time-dependent Lipschitz velocity and a limsup result. The entry fixes the smooth unit ABC field and asks for a positive liminf. |
| 229: one cubic-NLS orbit with unbounded high norm | Herr–Kwak, [*Global well-posedness of the cubic nonlinear Schrödinger equation on T²*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-026-01418-4) (2026): low-regularity global existence does not establish high-norm growth. Finite growth constructions with initial data depending on the target size also do not provide one unbounded trajectory. |
| 230: general renormalized Boltzmann uniqueness | Galeano Andrades–Almanza Caro, [*Uniqueness of solutions to Boltzmann equations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08011) (2020 preprint), Theorem 2.1: additional collision-contraction assumptions include a constant strictly below one. The finite mass/energy/entropy assumptions do not supply those hypotheses. An–Lee’s [2026 primary article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40818-026-00243-5), introduction, continues to state general renormalized uniqueness as open. |
| 231: boundary-thermostated anharmonic chain | Canestrari–Liverani–Olla, [*Heat equation from a deterministic dynamics*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-026-01429-1) (2026): the model includes an external chaotic force. The retained chain has Hamiltonian bulk evolution and only two boundary reservoirs. |
| 232: mechanical-entropy BV blowup for polytropic pressure | Krupa, [*Non-uniqueness and BV blowup for vacuously Liu-admissible solutions to p-system via computer-assisted proof*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00021-026-01018-5) (2026): the abstract explicitly distinguishes its solutions from those obeying the natural strictly convex entropy. The September preprint above also does not supply the retained polytropic, mechanical-entropy scenario. |
| 234: nonlinear stability of pure-gravity solitary waves | Rousset–Sun, [*Transverse linear stability of one-dimensional solitary gravity water waves*](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.11115v1) (2024 preprint; 2025 publication): introduction distinguishes linear results, capillary conditional stability, and nonlinear results for reduced equations from the full pure-gravity nonlinear question. |
| 242: cooling-profile uniqueness at arbitrary restitution | Mischler–Mouhot, [*Stability, convergence to self-similarity and elastic limit for the Boltzmann equation for inelastic hard spheres*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701449), Theorem 1.1(i): uniqueness is established near the elastic limit. Results for one-dimensional models, Maxwell molecules, or forced gases do not settle arbitrary restitution for the displayed three-dimensional hard-sphere equation. |
| 243: degree-one Bloch-wall attainment | Döring–Ignat, [*Asymmetric domain walls of small angle in soft ferromagnetic films*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.2382), Theorem 1: small-angle asymptotics describe the Néel-wall class. They do not prove attainment in the prescribed degree-one Bloch-wall class. |
