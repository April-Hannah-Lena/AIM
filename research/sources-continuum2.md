# Source map for problems 151–175

Checked on **2026-09-08**. The pages draw on monographs, published problem lists, research surveys and original papers. Each page records the precise model, direct references, search topics and the limits of the partial results found. “No later resolution located” describes this literature search, not a certification that no proof exists.

| Entries | Source and verified location | Use and current-status distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 151 | Isett, [*On the endpoint regularity in Onsager’s conjecture*](https://msp.org/apde/2024/17-6/apde-v17-n6-p09-s.pdf) (2024), introduction | Exact Hölder endpoint; separated from solved subcritical Onsager and conservation with a vanishing critical Besov tail. |
| 152–153 | Khesin–Misiołek–Shnirelman, [*Geometric Hydrodynamics in Open Problems*](https://www.math.toronto.edu/khesin/papers/Problems_in_GHD_ARMA.pdf) (2023), §5.2 Problem 8(i) and §5.3 Problem 10 | Global endpoint surjectivity and higher-genus material recurrence. Arnold–Khesin’s [1998 monograph](https://doi.org/10.1007/b97593), Chapter I, provides the Euler–geodesic framework. |
| 154 | Zelik, [*Attractors. Then and now*](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/rm10095) (2023), inertial-manifold discussion | Ordinary two-dimensional Navier–Stokes remains distinguished from hyperviscous results, including Guo’s [2024 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14642). |
| 155 | Lazarovici–Pickl, [*A mean field limit for the Vlasov–Poisson system*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04608) (2017), introduction and Theorem 2.1 | Uncut repulsive Coulomb particles; theorem uses a cutoff. Griffin-Pickering’s [2026 ionic-model paper](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/52877), introduction, retains the point-charge difficulty. |
| 156 | Duerinckx–Le Bihan, [*Lenard-Balescu thermalization: rigorous derivation from a toy model*](https://mitia.duerinckx.web.ulb.be/publications-DLB25.pdf) (2025), §1.1, equations (1.1)–(1.3) | Full interacting Gibbs background and screened diffusion on the kinetic time scale; the proved toy model and February 2026 free-gas theorem are partial results. |
| 157 | Winter, [*Convergence to the Landau equation from the truncated BBGKY hierarchy in the weak-coupling limit*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.05021) (2021), introduction and equations (1.1)–(1.5) | Full nonlinear particle limit, formulated on a finite periodic volume. The source describes a whole-space particle ensemble; the page states the finite-volume formulation explicitly. Truncated hierarchies and intermediate kinetic equations are not the target. |
| 158 | Tsai, [*Lectures on Navier–Stokes Equations*](https://doi.org/10.1090/gsm/192) (2018), Conjecture 2.5; Chae, [CMP 407 (2026), article 53](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-026-05555-y), introduction and Theorem 1.1 | Finite-Dirichlet stationary Liouville question without extra decay, integrability or head-pressure assumptions. |
| 159 | Korobkov–Ren, [*Leray’s plane stationary solutions at small Reynolds numbers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.08898) (2021), introduction and Theorem 1 | Prescribed far-field velocity for arbitrary Reynolds number. Nontriviality and small-Reynolds existence do not settle this formulation. |
| 160–161 | Hencl, [*Ball–Evans approximation problem: recent progress and open problems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01336) (2025), §2, Open problems 1 and 4, functional $E_1$ | Three-dimensional Sobolev approximation and planar compressible-energy approximation. Checked the [2026 piecewise-affine smoothing theorem](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12220-026-02398-w), which assumes an approximation stage still missing in the general problem. |
| 162 | Ball, [*Some Open Problems in Elasticity*](https://doi.org/10.1007/0-387-21791-6_1) (2002), Problem 16 and equation (4.3) | General cubic-to-tetragonal quasiconvex hull. Ball–Galanopoulou’s [July 2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12572), introduction and Taylor-bound discussion, distinguishes available diagonal-strain information. |
| 163 | Lamy–Marconi, [*Rectifiability of entropy productions for weak solutions to the 2D eikonal equation with supercritical regularity*](https://doi.org/10.4171/RMI/1614) (2026), §1, equations (1.2), (1.4), Theorem 1.1 | Full entropy-concentration question with $C^{1,1}$ entropies. Its new supercritical result uses the narrower odd-entropy-plus-constant class. De Lellis–Otto’s jump-set rectifiability is separated from concentration on that set. |
| 164 | Firester–Tsiamis–Wang, [*Stability inequalities for one-phase cones*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16966) (2026), introduction and Theorem 1.1 | Critical dimension remains between five and seven; symmetry-restricted advances do not decide it. The definition excludes the zero minimizer. |
| 165–166 | Mantegazza–Novaga–Pluda–Schulze, [*Evolution of networks with multiple junctions*](https://people.dm.unipi.it/novaga/papers/mantegazza/tripunto/network-survey.pdf), Astérisque 452 (2024), linked author text: Open problems 9.1 and 7.25, §15 items 2–3 | Multiplicity and uniqueness of tangent shapes are separate singularity questions. The cited numbering is that of the linked text; older drafts have different numbering. |
| 167 | Doering–Tobasco, [*On the Optimal Design of Wall-to-Wall Heat Transport*](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.21832) (2019), main bounds; Kumar, [three-dimensional branching-pipe construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03367) (2022), introduction | Remaining logarithmic gap in two-dimensional enstrophy-constrained transport. Three-dimensional branching closes the gap in a different admissible class. |
| 168 | Ding–Kerswell, [JFM 889 (2020), A33](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.03376), introduction and conclusion; Ding–Wen–Li, [June 2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.14138v1), introduction and formulation | Published optimal heat-scaling question made precise for fixed finite Prandtl number, aspect ratio and no-slip walls. A model extremum is not a matching bound for actual buoyant solutions. |
| 169 | Ehrnström–Mæhlen–Varholm, [AIHPC 42 (2025), 125–162](https://doi.org/10.4171/AIHPC/104), equations (1.2)–(1.3), §4, Remark 4.8 | Extreme solitary existence for a specific bidirectional Whitham system; separated from small solitary waves and known unidirectional extreme waves. |
| 170 | Escobedo–Mischler–Rodríguez Ricard, [AIHPC 22 (2005), 99–125](https://ems.press/journals/aihpc/articles/4076412), self-similar-profile results; Niethammer–Throm–Velázquez, [2017 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.03361v3), main uniqueness theorem | Brownian profile uniqueness after fixing mass. Near-constant-kernel theorems require a perturbation smallness condition absent for the Brownian kernel. |
| 171 | Gasull–Santana, [*A note on Hilbert 16th Problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13465) (2024), §1; Ilyashenko, [centennial survey](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-02-00946-1) (2002) | Uniform quadratic limit-cycle bound; finite cycle count for each fixed vector field is already known. |
| 172 | Benjamini–Shamov–Weiss, [*Non-colliding billiards in the plane*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23575v2) (May 2026), §1 and version history | Stationary marked point process with bounded distinct velocities and all-time separation. The page preserves the stronger positive-lower-speed and isotropy conditions discussed in the initial version and checks the revised author list. |
| 173 | Buckmaster–Vicol, [*Convex integration and phenomenologies in turbulence*](https://cims.nyu.edu/~vicol/BV2.pdf) (2019), §8, Problem 10; Protas, [August 2026 survey](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04859v1), §5.2 | Fixed smooth large-scale forcing and bounded mean energy. Existing viscosity-dependent forcing constructions do not supply the requested example. |
| 174 | Jerrard–Seis, [*On the vortex filament conjecture for Euler flows*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.00227) (2017), main conditional theorem; Guo–Jeong–Zhao, [*Global dynamics of a single vortex ring*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20131) (2026), main theorem | General closed filament evolution without a concentration assumption. The new axisymmetric no-swirl ring result is a symmetry-restricted advance. |
| 175 | Jeong–Yao–Zhou, [*Superlinear gradient growth for 2D Euler equation without boundary*](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.15739v1) (2025), §1.1 | One fixed smooth periodic datum with sustained double-exponential gradient growth. Finite-duration constructions, lower-regularity data and boundary-assisted growth are separated explicitly. |
