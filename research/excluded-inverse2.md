# Exclusions and corrected status distinctions for the second inverse/control batch

Checked **2026-09-08**. The following are not counted among problems 121–145.

| Candidate or misleading apparent resolution | Reason and primary evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Every sub-Riemannian minimizer is smooth | False: Chitour et al., [*Not all sub-Riemannian minimizing geodesics are smooth*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18920) (2025), Theorem 1.1. Entry 126 asks only for $C^1$. |
| Every analytic sub-Riemannian manifold is nonbranching | False: Rossi–Schiavoni Piazza–Socionovo, [arXiv:2603.23068](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23068) (2026), Theorem 1.1. Entry 127 keeps the expressly open equiregular case. |
| Viterbo's general volume-capacity inequality for convex bodies | Counterexample: P. Haim-Kislev and Y. Ostrover, [*A counterexample to Viterbo's conjecture*](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2026.203.2.5), Annals 203 (2026), 603–622. |
| Merely proving at least $n$ periodic orbits on a smooth convex Hamiltonian energy surface | Established by Çineli–Ginzburg–Gürel, [arXiv:2410.13093](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13093), Theorem A. Entry 134 asks the stronger finite-orbit dichotomy. |
| Existence of a noncollision singularity in a four-body Newtonian system | Established by Xue, [Acta Mathematica 224 (2020)](https://doi.org/10.4310/ACTA.2020.v224.n2.a2). Entry 133 concerns the measure of bad initial data for five or more bodies. |
| Two-dimensional disc minimal filling treated as a fresh unresolved question | Ivanov proves the Finsler-disc comparison in [arXiv:0910.2257](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.2257). Entry 123 allows competing fillings of arbitrary topology. The closely related filling-area conjecture was not counted separately. |
| Global null control for the pure three-equation cubic cascade | Coron–Guilleron, [*Control of Three Heat Equations Coupled with Two Cubic Nonlinearities*](https://doi.org/10.1137/15M1041201) (2017), proves the cascade result. This differs from the growing scalar reaction in 147. |
| Existence of weakly mixing polygonal billiards | Chaika–Forni, [Annals 203 (2026), 695–735](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2026.203.3.1), proves a dense $G_\delta$ family. Entry 144 asks for a nonergodic irrational polygon. |
| Positive-entropy perturbations of the identity among arbitrary smooth area-preserving maps | Berger–Turaev, [Advances in Mathematics 349 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2019.04.002), resolves Herman's question. Entry 145 requires realizability by a perturbation of the billiard boundary. |
| Full Gaussian interference capacity used without resolving recent proof claims | Conservative exclusion: A. K. Khandani, [arXiv:2501.14941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14941) (2025), claims optimality in a weak-interference setting. The claim was not independently adjudicated, so this candidate was replaced by the binary multiplying channel. |

Two claimed solutions were retained only as explicitly qualified status evidence:

- Alexakis–Lassas, [arXiv:2510.27085](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27085), was withdrawn January 6, 2026 because a sign error affected the proof. It does not remove entry 122.
- Tibboel, [arXiv:2212.09718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09718), was withdrawn March 22, 2024 with an incorrect-main-theorem/proof-error notice. It does not remove entry 131.

Other scope checks: finite-dimensional uniqueness of positive heat controls does not prove continuum uniqueness; “Cover's relay problem” saturation-threshold results do not give the entire Gaussian relay capacity; non-adaptive zero-error two-way coding does not give adaptive vanishing-error capacity; spatial-memory billiards change the model; numeric positive Lyapunov exponents do not certify the classical Hénon SRB conjecture. Existing entries 048–072 were checked for overlaps, particularly global tomography, simplicity, billiard integrability and the standard map.
