# Spectral candidates excluded during the status review

Checked on 2026-09-08. An exclusion means that the collection does not present the candidate as open. Recent preprints are identified as claims of resolution; their proofs have not been independently refereed here.

| Candidate | Evidence and reason for exclusion |
| --- | --- |
| Ashbaugh–Benguria reciprocal sums of Neumann eigenvalues | Y. He, Y. Li and Q. Tang, [A proof of the Ashbaugh–Benguria conjecture for reciprocal sums of Neumann eigenvalues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08271), submitted 6 June 2026, claims the sharp ball inequality in every dimension. |
| Ashbaugh–Benguria reciprocal Dirichlet gaps | Y. Li, Q. Tang and H. Zhang, [The Ashbaugh–Benguria reciprocal-gap conjecture for Dirichlet eigenvalues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01135), submitted 1 July 2026, claims the full inequality and equality classification. |
| Ashbaugh's higher-index Dirichlet PPW ratio | Q. Tang's [manuscript and exact-arithmetic verification repository](https://github.com/QuanyuTang/dirichlet-eigenvalue-ratio-verification), first public version 11 August 2026, gives explicit annular counterexamples. The repository says its arXiv submission is on moderation hold; this is a public manuscript claim, not a journal publication. It concerns higher-index ratios, not the first-two-eigenvalue polygonal conjecture in problem 020. |
| Closed nodal-line conjecture for the third Dirichlet eigenfunction | Z. Deng, [A Counterexample to the Closed Nodal-Line Conjecture for the Third Eigenfunction of the Planar Dirichlet Laplacian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27535), submitted 27 August 2026, constructs a smooth connected domain with simple third eigenvalue and an entirely interior nodal set. This does not settle the third-eigenvalue shape optimization in problem 005. |
| Multidimensional complex-potential Lieb–Thirring generalization of Demuth–Hansmann–Katriel | S. Bögli, S. Petpradittha and F. Štampach, [On Lieb–Thirring inequalities for multidimensional Schrödinger operators with complex potentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02192), Integral Equations and Operator Theory 97, 25 (2025), gives counterexamples. Problem 018 instead concerns the real-potential sharp constant in a specific dimension and exponent. |
| Convex hot spots in unrestricted dimension | J. de Dios Pont, [Convex sets can have interior hot spots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06344), preprint (2024), constructs counterexamples in sufficiently large dimension. Problem 003 retains the explicitly open planar class. |
| Pólya's conjecture restricted to higher-dimensional Neumann balls | N. Filonov, M. Levitin, I. Polterovich and D. Sher, [Pólya's conjecture for higher-dimensional Neumann balls](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29305), submitted 31 July 2026, proves that remaining ball case and acknowledges Y. Li's independent proof. Problem 001 concerns arbitrary Lipschitz domains. |
| Payne nodal-line conjecture on doubly connected planar domains | P. Freitas and R. Leylekian, [Payne’s nodal line conjecture fails on doubly-connected planar domains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24436), preprint (2025), gives doubly connected counterexamples. |

Other solved restrictions are described within their corresponding entries: triangles for polygonal PPW; triangles and quadrilaterals for polygonal Faber–Krahn; dimensions two and three for clamped plates; and analytic boundaries for exponential Steklov decay. The simply connected Payne conjecture was not selected because the search also surfaced a proof claim whose status could not be confidently adjudicated within this review.
