## Conflicting proof claim retained with explicit disclosure

For 269, [Mustafa Kesal's 2015 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.01828) claims a proof of the most informative Boolean function conjecture. The much later specialist paper by [Chen–Gohari–Nair, revised August 5, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10019), still states it as Conjecture 1 and develops partial results. The entry explicitly reports this discrepancy and follows the later specialist literature; it does not assert that this audit has identified an error in the older manuscript.

## New bounds distinguished from solutions

The 2026 papers of [Nishioka](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01754), [Deng](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04591), [Tandon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30273), and [Zeng](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01284) were inspected for their actual conclusions. They provide improved bounds or constructions for 251, 252, 270 and 272. None claims the exact optimum requested in the corresponding entry.
