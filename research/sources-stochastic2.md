# Source map: stochastic models and statistical mechanics (176–200)

Reviewed on **8 September 2026**. These entries widen the applied collection into growth, transport, ecology, chemical kinetics, and disordered matter. They contain no numerical linear algebra. A source locator identifies the question or the nearest explicit general formulation; each entry explains any narrower case selected.

## Specific papers and surveys

- **184:** Losev–Smirnov, [*How Long Are the Arms in DBM?* (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-025-05276-8), §1, equation (1) and the following growth-exponent prediction; compared with Kesten's DLA bound.
- **185–186:** Campos–Ramírez, [*Ellipticity criteria for ballistic behavior of random walks in random environment* (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-013-0527-7), Conjecture 1.1; Zeitouni's [author survey](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~zeitouni/pdf/rwrereview.pdf), Conjecture 4.2. Guerra–Ramírez's later theorem was checked to distinguish the solved ballisticity-condition equivalence.
- **187:** Hoffman–Johnson–Junge, [*The density conjecture for activated random walk*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01731), and Forien's [later proof on the line](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02579). The two-dimensional box question is not their one-dimensional theorem.
- **191:** Lyons–Peres explicitly record generating-set invariance for the Liouville property. Zheng’s [ICM 2022 survey](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~tiz161/Zheng_RWsurvey.pdf), §2.1, and Matte Bon–Nekrashevych–Zheng’s [2025 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14545), Theorem 1.1, distinguish broader stability questions and known group subclasses. Nontransitive-network counterexamples are outside this statement.
- **194:** Michelen–Perkins, [*Potential-weighted connective constants and uniqueness of Gibbs measures*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01094), §1, explicitly unresolved continuum nonuniqueness. Guo–Jerrum provide a complementary low-density sampling result.
- **196:** Dario–Harel–Peled, [*Quantitative Disorder Effects in Low-Dimensional Spin Systems* (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-024-05081-9), §§2.3.2 and 9, and Arguin–Damron's ground-state analysis.
- **197:** Mueller–Mytnik–Perkins, [*Nonuniqueness for a parabolic SPDE with three-quarter-minus-epsilon Hölder diffusion coefficients*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2767), the explicit endpoint question after Theorem 1.1; compared with Mytnik–Perkins and Han's August 2026 nondegenerate weak-uniqueness theorem.

Recent comparisons include the April 2026 rotationally invariant FPP variance result, January 2026 numerical Ising roughening study, May 2026 Ising phase-ordering paper, and August 2026 stochastic-heat uniqueness paper. Their different models, initial conditions, or notions of uniqueness are recorded on the relevant pages. The source map records targeted reading and status searches; it does not claim that every cited book or every proof was read in full.
