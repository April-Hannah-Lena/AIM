Resolution comparison and limitations
-------------------------------------
At the manuscript's indices 32, 64, 128 and 192, the relative changes in center
magnitude between the two orders were approximately 4.72e-14, 1.05e-11,
8.42e-9 and 4.81e-6. At index 192 the eigenvalue changed by 1.59e-8.

Values near the truncation edge are less stable: at index 256 the order-256
eigenvalue differs from the order-384 value by about 1.79323, and its center
magnitude differs by about 164 percent. The full CSVs therefore should not
be interpreted as uniformly resolved continuum data.

All of these computations use finite truncations of q and floating-point
arithmetic. No certified enclosure or continuum convergence rate is supplied.
The infinite-dimensional result depends on the exact proof and full infinite
series, independently of this numerical supplement.
