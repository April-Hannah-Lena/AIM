# Applied-analysis candidates excluded or narrowed

Reviewed on **8 September 2026**. These decisions are outside the count of 100. A solution to one model, dimension or data class does not automatically resolve a neighboring problem.

| Candidate or broader formulation | Evidence found | Editorial decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finite-time blow-up for the spatially homogeneous Landau equation with Coulomb interactions and regular rapidly decaying data | Nestor Guillen and Luis Silvestre, [The Landau equation does not blow up](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09420), Theorem 1.2, gives global classical solutions for a stated class including Maxwellian-bounded data with finite Fisher information. Their [2025 exposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05167) explains the resolution. | Excluded this old regularity question. Entry 076 treats the different, spatially inhomogeneous Boltzmann equation. |
| Bourgain's slicing conjecture | Boaz Klartag and Joseph Lehec, [Affirmative Resolution of Bourgain's Slicing Problem using Guan's Bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15044), posted December 2024 and published in Geometric and Functional Analysis in 2025, establishes the dimension-independent hyperplane-section bound. | Excluded. The dimension-independent Poincaré inequality in entry 094 is the stronger KLS question; slicing alone does not prove it. |
| Global smoothness for all energy-supercritical defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equations | Frank Merle, Pierre Raphaël, Igor Rodnianski and Jérémie Szeftel, [On blow up for the energy super critical defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equations](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-021-01067-9), Inventiones mathematicae 227 (2022), constructs smooth finite-time blow-up solutions in a range of dimensions and powers. | Excluded the universal global-regularity formulation. This does not settle the real scalar wave equation in entry 079. |
| Global regularity for all complex-valued supercritical defocusing wave equations | Feng Shao, Dongyi Wei and Zhifei Zhang, [On blow-up for the supercritical defocusing nonlinear wave equation](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2025.7), 2025, constructs singularities for complex-valued equations in specified dimensions and parameter ranges. | Entry 079 explicitly asks about a real scalar equation in three dimensions. |
| The symmetric Mahler conjecture in three dimensions | Hiroshi Iriyeh and Masataka Shibata, [Symmetric Mahler's conjecture for the volume product in the 3-dimensional case](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.01749), published in Duke Mathematical Journal in 2020, proves the three-dimensional case. | Excluded dimension three; entry 088 asks for dimension four. |
| Radial soliton resolution for energy-critical waves | Jacek Jendrej and Andrew Lawrie, [Soliton resolution for the energy-critical nonlinear wave equation in the radial case](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40818-023-00159-4), 2023, resolves the radial problem. | Entry 080 specifies the general nonradial, all-time statement. |
| Kelvin's original proposed foam as the unrestricted optimum | The discussion in Cesaroni and Novaga, [Minimal periodic foams with fixed inradius](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07534), reviews the distinction between general equal-volume foams and lattice translates of one cell. | Entry 086 is expressly the lattice-periodic single-cell problem. It does not repeat the disproved assertion that Kelvin's original foam minimizes among all equal-volume foams. |

Searches used problem names and formulations with `proof`, `counterexample`, `resolved`, `open`, and recent years. Further distinctions and partial results are recorded on the individual problem pages, including boundary versus whole-space fluid singularities, discrete versus continuum thin-film positivity, and triangular versus square-lattice percolation.
