## Review notes

The 25 statements were checked against their mathematical models and against the existing collection. Similar-looking pairs have different objects: 252 covers unit-length curves, whereas 272 covers arbitrary diameter-one sets; 256 maximizes volume, whereas 257 asks for a surface-area inequality. Different billiard reflection laws are defined explicitly. Problem 273 concerns exact decidability of continuous-time reachability, not numerical linear algebra.
