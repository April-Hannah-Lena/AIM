# 261 — Rigidity of a totally integrable Minkowski billiard in its own unit ball

**Area:** Finsler geometry / anisotropic ray mechanics

**Status:** 🔵 OPEN

**Last checked:** 2026-09-13

## Problem statement

Let $N:\mathbb R^2\to[0,\infty)$ be a norm, smooth away from zero, with positive-definite Hessian of $N^2$ there. Put $K=\{v:N(v)\leq1\}$ and parametrize $\partial K$ by $N$-arclength $\gamma(s)$. The billiard map on ordered distinct boundary points is generated by $L(s,t)=N(\gamma(t)-\gamma(s))$: successive parameters satisfy

$$
\partial_2L(s_{j-1},s_j)+\partial_1L(s_j,s_{j+1})=0.
$$

If its entire phase annulus is foliated by continuous invariant simple curves winding once around the annulus, must $N(v)=\sqrt{v^TQv}$ for a positive-definite symmetric matrix $Q$?

## Application

The boundary and propagation cost use the same anisotropy. The question tests whether complete organization of reflected rays can identify an underlying inner-product geometry.
