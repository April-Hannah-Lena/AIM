# 135 — Irrational ellipticity when a convex energy surface has finitely many orbits

**Area:** Hamiltonian mechanics / orbital stability

**Status:** 🔵 OPEN

**Last checked:** 2026-09-08

## Problem statement

Let $\Sigma\subset\mathbb R^{2n}$, $n\ge3$, be a smooth compact strictly convex energy hypersurface with positive definite second fundamental form. Its closed characteristics are closed integral curves of the one-dimensional kernel of the restricted symplectic form $\omega=\sum_j dx_j\wedge dy_j$.

Suppose there are only finitely many geometrically distinct simple closed characteristics. Must each be irrationally elliptic? Explicitly, is the derivative of its transverse Poincaré return map symplectically conjugate to a direct sum

$$
R(\theta_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus R(\theta_{n-1}),\qquad
R(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\ \sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \theta_j/(2\pi)\notin\mathbb Q?
$$

This includes diagonalizability and rules out roots of unity among the transverse multipliers.

## Application

The statement connects the number of periodic motions to their linear stability: a finite orbit family would force every orbit into a nonresonant elliptic form.

## References

1. L. Liu and Y. Ou, *Three elliptic closed characteristics on the non-degenerate compact convex hypersurfaces in R6* (2026), [arXiv:2603.12656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12656), §1, Conjecture 2 and Theorems 1.2 and 1.6. States the conjecture and proves partial stability results.
2. X. Li, H. Liu and W. Wang, *Two irrationally elliptic closed orbits of Reeb flows on the boundary of star-shaped domain in R2n* (2025), [arXiv:2510.06597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06597), introduction and main theorem. Establishes two such orbits under its finite-orbit hypotheses, rather than the property for every orbit.

## Status review

**Literature check:** Open in cited literature; no later resolution located.

The conjecture is known in $\mathbb R^4$. The March 2026 paper obtains three elliptic orbits in a nondegenerate six-dimensional case, with at least two irrationally elliptic; this is not the assertion for every orbit. Searches included “all closed characteristics finitely many irrationally elliptic conjecture 2026” and “Liu Ou 2603.12656 stability conjecture”. No complete higher-dimensional resolution was located.
