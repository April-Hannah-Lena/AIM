# 263 — Quadratic growth when a Hamiltonian map has infinitely many periodic orbits

**Area:** Hamiltonian dynamics / recurrent mechanical motion

## Problem statement

Let $(M^{2n},\omega)$ be a closed connected symplectic manifold and $\phi$ the time-one map of a smooth time-periodic Hamiltonian on $M$. Let $P(T)$ count distinct primitive periodic orbits of $\phi$ whose least integer period is at most $T$; points on the same orbit and repeated traversals are counted only once. Allow $P(T)=\infty$.

If $\phi$ has infinitely many periodic orbits, is

$$
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{\log P(T)}{\log T}\geq2
$$

always true, with $\log\infty=\infty$?

## Applied significance

The statement would give a universal lower rate at which distinct resonances appear as the observation period increases in periodically forced conservative systems.

## References

1. B. Albach, U. Hryniewicz and A. Oancea, contribution to *Open problems in billiards and quantitative symplectic geometry* (2026), [arXiv:2602.12896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12896), their section, Problem 1. Exact higher-dimensional growth question.
2. B. Albach, *Quadratic growth of geodesics on the two-sphere* (2025), [arXiv:2508.00147](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00147), introduction and results on symplectic maps of the sphere. Establishes a low-dimensional case and motivates the proposed growth threshold.
