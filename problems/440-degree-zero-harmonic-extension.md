# 440. Continuous harmonic extension of every degree-zero sphere boundary map

**Area:** Elliptic systems and orientational order

**Status:** 🔵 OPEN

**Last checked:** 2026-09-22
## Problem statement

Let $`B^3`$ be the unit ball and $`g\in C^\infty(S^2;S^2)`$ have topological degree zero. Must there exist

```math
u\in H^1(B^3;\mathbb R^3)\cap C(\overline B^3;S^2),\qquad u|_{S^2}=g,
```

such that $`-\Delta u=|\nabla u|^2u`$ distributionally in $`B^3`$? The conclusion asks for at least one continuous harmonic map; it does not require that map to minimize the Dirichlet energy among all Sobolev competitors.

## Application

Sphere-valued harmonic maps model equilibrium orientation fields, including the simplest liquid-crystal director model. The problem asks whether topologically compatible anchoring always permits an equilibrium without a point defect.

## Status review

**Literature check:** Open in cited literature; no later resolution located.

The 2023 formulation was checked against its full PDE and boundary assumptions. Review-date searches for Schoen’s continuous harmonic extension problem, degree-zero ball data, and later harmonic-map existence results located no general solution. Singular energy minimizers and nonexistence within an axial-symmetry class do not decide unrestricted existence.
