# 422. Uniqueness of the zero-average dispersion-managed soliton

**Area:** Nonlocal dispersive PDEs / optical pulse design

**Status:** 🔵 OPEN

**Last checked:** 2026-09-22

## Problem statement

Let $T_r=e^{ir\partial_x^2}$ be the free Schrödinger group on $L^2(\mathbb R;\mathbb C)$, and define
$$\mathcal Q(f)=\int_0^1\int_{\mathbb R}|T_rf(x)|^4\,dx\,dr,\qquad P=\sup_{\|f\|_2=1}\mathcal Q(f).$$
The supremum is finite and attained. Let $G$ be the group generated by phase rotations $f(x)\mapsto e^{i\theta}f(x)$, translations $f(x)\mapsto f(x-a)$, modulations $f(x)\mapsto e^{i\xi x}f(x)$, reflection $f(x)\mapsto f(-x)$, and dispersion-time reversal $f\mapsto T_{-1}\overline f$, with $\theta,a,\xi\in\mathbb R$. Each generator preserves $\mathcal Q$ and the mass. Are any two maximizers in the same $G$-orbit?
The dispersion profile is fixed to the uniform measure on $[0,1]$ and the average dispersion is zero. No uniqueness of arbitrary critical points is requested.

## Application

Maximizers generate ground-state optical pulses for a fiber with alternating positive and negative dispersion and zero net dispersion. Uniqueness would determine whether a fixed pulse energy selects one basic optimal pulse shape, apart from its phase, translation, frequency and reversal symmetries.

## Status review

**Literature check:** Open in cited literature; no later resolution located.

The archived primary research-program description identifies ground-state uniqueness as open, and the 2012 paper supplies the precise zero-average variational model and continuous symmetries. Reflection and dispersion-time reversal are included explicitly to quotient all evident symmetries of this uniform-profile functional. The SIAM uniqueness theorem assumes positive average dispersion and a sufficiently small perturbation parameter; it does not apply to the zero-average functional here. Searches through 22 September 2026 for zero-dispersion ground-state uniqueness found no resolution. Setwise orbital stability and uniqueness of evolution for a fixed initial state are different results.
